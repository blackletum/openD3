# Retained Doom 3 menus

14 and 15 November 2026. First twelve increments of the vector replacement that the
[visual design specification](../ui-visual-design.md) describes and the
[Interface Atlas](atlas/opend3-interface-atlas.html) draws. It follows the way
openQ4 replaced its menus: retained (`.rui`) documents generated by a script
from the specification, presented by the session over the stock menu, with the
stock menu still providing every page that has no retained document yet.

It is a preview. `ui_retained` stays 0 by default, and launches keep pinning it
to 0, because the retained HUD has no Doom 3 documents. `+set ui_retained 1` selects what is listed here.

## How a document is presented

The stock menu stays loaded and active underneath, as in openQ4. Three things
differ for Doom 3, each at an `OPEND3` site in `src/framework/Session_menu.cpp`.

- **The stock menu draws the scene.** Space, the stars, the planet and its halo,
  the scan lines, the grid, the intro video and the opening fade are real
  imagery or stock behaviour the specification keeps (bitmap exceptions,
  section 6). `Session_DrawRetainedScene` redraws the stock menu with its
  furniture hidden (`PrimaryMenuParent`, `DoomLogo`, `DoomLogo2`, `ToolTip1`,
  `ToolTip2`, `OuterGlowBar`) and without its pointer, and the retained document
  draws its furniture over that. The planet's fly-in and rotation, the music and
  the id logo therefore need no retained counterpart. `UI_SetLegacyWindowHidden`
  and `UI_SetLegacyCursorHidden` are the two engine additions; a hidden window's
  timelines keep running.
- **The home presents while the stock menu rests at home.** Doom 3's menu names
  its page in `desktop::selected` (0 at home) and holds `desktop::doomme` at 0
  while the id logo plays. The retained home presents when both say so.
- **A page without a document is the stock page.** The home runs the stock
  button's own action (`RETAINED_HANDOFFS`: `OptionsBtnText`, `LoadBtnText`,
  `SaveGameBtnText`, `MultiplayerBtnText`, `ModsBtnText`, `CreditsBtnText`) and
  leaves at once. The stock menu then plays its opening from the same
  composition the home drew. Back on the stock page sets `desktop::selected` to
  0 immediately and plays its closing choreography; the home takes the screen
  back 450 ms later. When a retained page document exists (`loadgame.rui`
  and `savegame.rui` today; `credits.rui`, `settings.rui` and
  `multiplayer.rui` have routes waiting), the session's existing page route
  takes it instead.
- **A retained page unfolds over the home.** The home keeps drawing behind
  the page as its departure left it (the logo fades, its hint line goes). The
  page presents 50 ms after the choice (`RETAINED_PAGE_HANDOFF_MSEC`; openQ4
  waits 550 ms) and plays section 8.2's construction itself: bridge, plate,
  flair, then content at 400 ms. It draws the nav button that opened it again
  in the same place as the way back, and the home hides its own copy
  (`carry_loadGame`, `carry_saveGame`).

- **OPTIONS' list belongs to the home.** CONTROLS and GAME OPTIONS open their
  retained pages over the home, as the Load Game page opens; the list stays
  open behind them and is there again when a page closes. SYSTEM opens
  through the engine's own route (`OpenSystemSettings`), which makes the page
  the settings service's owner; the home keeps drawing behind it and takes
  the screen back when the page dismisses itself. DEFAULTS asks in the
  home's own dialog (`defaultsModalShow`), as the stock RESET ALL DEFAULTS
  dialog asks over the stock list, with CANCEL focused; its YES is the
  session's `defaultsYes`, which does what the stock dialog's YES does:
  `exec cvar_restart` through the stock menu's command handler (every CVar
  to its default, then `default.cfg` and `setMachineSpec`) and `loadBinds`.
  `ui_retained` is put back afterwards: the interface choice is not one of
  the control, game and system options the dialog names. Where a page's
  document cannot present, SYSTEM falls back to the stock menu: the session
  presses the stock OPTIONS button and, 720 ms later when the stock list has
  unfolded, the stock item (`retainedStockFollow`). The stock menu hands
  back when its list closes.

- **Mods and credits use openQ4's routes unchanged.** MODS publishes the
  installed mods into the title document and raises its `modsModalShow`
  (`PublishRetainedMods`, `SelectRetainedMod`); the panel is the stock Mods
  page drawn in the title. CREDITS presents `credits.rui` as a page; the
  title's furniture leaves for it (`carry_credits`). The page carries no
  names: it declares a `credits.<window>` state for each line and the session
  fills it from the stock menu's credits window of that name
  (`PublishRetainedCredits`), so the words still come from the installation.

- **MULTIPLAYER's list belongs to the title.** FIND MULTIPLAYER GAME opens
  the Game Browser through openQ4's Join Game route (`OpenRetainedJoinGame`,
  `PublishRetainedJoinGame`, `HandleRetainedJoinGameRequest`) and asks for a
  new server list, as the stock item does. The browser is a page of its own
  over the stock scene, without the home behind it; MAIN MENU and Back
  return to the home (`joinBack`). CREATE SERVER opens the Create Server
  page through openQ4's route as well (`OpenRetainedCreateServer`,
  `PublishRetainedCreateServer`, `PublishRetainedCreateMaps`,
  `HandleRetainedCreateServerRequest`), a page of the same kind. Each
  page's other tab returns to the home and opens the other page
  (`joinCreate`, `createBrowser`). Where a page's document cannot present,
  the session presses the stock MULTIPLAYER button and then the stock
  list's item (`retainedStockFollow`). MULTIPLAYER SETTINGS is a panel of
  the title, as the stock page is a panel of the stock home and as MODS
  is: the session fills in the player's name and the data rate's name and
  raises the title's `mpSettingsModalShow`; the list stays open behind it.

- **The death screen keeps the stock scene too.** When the player has died
  and the level is unloaded, `OpenRestartMenu` presents
  `restartmenu.rui` and opens the stock `guis/restart.gui` beside it. The
  stock screen goes on drawing its fire, faces, blood, pentagrams, logo and
  washes with its three choices and its load panel hidden
  (`RETAINED_DEATH_FURNITURE`), and the document draws the choices over
  it. The stock layout patch already keeps the logo and the choices on the
  centred 4:3 canvas, where the document puts them. The requests are
  openQ4's: `deathRestart` loads the checkpoint or the map's autosave, or
  restarts the map where there is neither; `deathLoad` leaves for the title
  and opens the Load Game page there; `deathMenu` leaves for the title. A
  press plays the stock screen's own `guisounds_ping2`.

- **A loading screen keeps its level's picture.** Each level has its own
  stock screen (`guis/map/<map>.gui`), all of one build. Where that screen
  has the word LOADING, a title and the bottom band,
  `SelectRetainedLoadingGui` presents `guis/loading/loading.rui` over it:
  the stock screen goes on drawing its picture, its scan lines and its
  opening fade with the rest hidden (`RETAINED_LOADING_FURNITURE`), and one
  document draws the bands, the word, the bar and the text for every
  level. The session reads the level's words and the bottom band's place
  from the stock screen's own windows and publishes them (`load.title`,
  `load.subtitle`, `load.briefing`, `load.text`, and in u `load.band_y`,
  `load.step_x`, `load.title_y`), so a multiplayer map's lower band and
  lone title and a long title's later step need no variants. The engine's
  `map_loading` drives the bar, clamped. openQ4's loading card extras
  (phases, tips, the arsenal, the server roster) are off for Doom 3. The
  first level's screen, the hell level's and the fallback are built
  differently and stay stock.

- **The in-game multiplayer menu is the game's, covered.** The game keeps
  its menu (`guis/mpmain.gui`): its state and its commands stay there, and
  `mp_escape.rui` covers it while it is open (`UpdateRetainedMultiplayer`).
  openQ4's game answers a cover protocol and publishes the card's state;
  Doom 3's game does neither, so the session covers the menu whenever it
  is the active GUI and copies what the card shows from the menu's own
  state (`Session_CopyGameMenuState`: `readyon`, `ui_ready`, `spectext`,
  `teamon`, `team`, `voteon`, `messagemode`). Doom 3's game also draws its
  menu itself, so a covered menu is hidden (`UI_SetLegacyWindowHidden` on
  its `Desktop`), not merely skipped. A request is the stock menu's own
  command, sent to the game with the menu's click. The card has every
  panel but System, which belongs to the engine's settings service and
  its one page: that tab uncovers the stock menu and presses its tab of
  that name (`mpStockPage`, `RETAINED_MP_STOCK_PAGES`), and the stock menu
  keeps the screen until it closes. The card stands where the stock menu
  stands, on the centred 4:3 canvas, so the hand-off does not move the
  strip.
  The Multiplayer panel is the title's Multiplayer Settings again
  (`mpRateNext`, `mpName`, and the `in_alwaysRun` and `ui_skin` CVar
  states). Options sets its CVars through `settings.player.set`. Controls
  uses the CONTROLS page's binding route: `RetainedControlsKey` takes the
  card as its host while it covers the menu, before the card sees the
  key, and there Delete and Backspace clear a binding only while a binding
  row has the focus, because the card also has text fields. New Vote
  follows the game's own panel: the card shows which types the server
  allows (`vote.on<n>`, `vote.allowed`), `mpVoteType` lists a type's
  choices from the game's menu (the four game types, `kickChoices`, or
  the game's map list after its `MAPScan`), `mpVoteItem` chooses one (a
  map through the game's list, which finds its picture), and `mpCallVote`
  sets `voteIndex` and the value where the game reads them and sends
  `callVote`.

A mod whose main menu lacks one of those stock buttons keeps its own menu.

## Session requests

A document asks the session through `session.menu` actions. Beyond openQ4's
verbs, openD3 adds:

| Request | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `newGame0` to `newGame3` | Sets the stock menu's `skill` and runs its `startGame` (`game/mars_city1`, or `game/erebus1` when `d3xp` is the game). `newGame3` plays the locked sound while `g_nightmare` is off |
| `optionsControls`, `optionsGame` | Present the CONTROLS or GAME OPTIONS page over the home; the stock page where its document cannot present |
| `optionsSystem` | Opens the retained SYSTEM page; the stock one where it cannot present |
| `optionsDefaults` | Raises the home's `defaultsModalShow` |
| `defaultsYes` | The dialog's YES: `exec cvar_restart` and `loadBinds` as the stock dialog runs them, `ui_retained` kept, then `defaultsModalHide` |
| `gameName` | Sets `ui_name` from the page's `game.name` |
| `mpBrowser` | Opens the Game Browser and asks for a new server list; the stock browser where it cannot present |
| `mpCreate` | Opens the Create Server page; the stock one where it cannot present |
| `mpSettings` | Publishes `mpsettings.name` and `mpsettings.rate_name` to the title and raises its `mpSettingsModalShow` |
| `mpRateNext` | Steps `net_clientMaxRate` through the stock rates (16000, 25600, 10000) and back to the rate the player had if it was none of them |
| `mpName` | Sets `ui_name` from the title's `mpsettings.name` |
| `mpReady`, `mpSpectate`, `mpTeamToggle` | In the in-game multiplayer menu: the game's `readytoggle`, `spectate`, `teamtoggle` |
| `mpChatMode` | Flips the game menu's `messagemode` (Say Global or Say Team) |
| `mpChat` | Puts the card's `mp.chat` in the game menu's `chattext` and sends `chatmessage` |
| `mpClick` | Plays the menu's click for a tab that only changes the card's panel |
| `mpVoteType`, `mpVoteItem` | New Vote: list the chosen type's choices (`vote.pick_type`); choose one (`vote.pick_item`) |
| `mpCallVote` | New Vote: hand the game the type and its value and call the vote |
| `joinCreate`, `createBrowser` | From one multiplayer page: back to the home, then `mpCreate` or `mpBrowser` |
| `createGameTypeNext`, `createRateNext` | Step Gametype or the server rate to its next value, as the stock choices step |
| `createReloadEngine <0 or 1>` | Sets `net_serverReloadEngine`, the one stock advanced option openQ4's route lacked |
| `soundForward`, `soundBack`, `soundApply`, `soundError`, `soundLocked` | Play section 8.7's sounds for a document's own moves that send nothing else (opening or closing a list or a dialog) |

The Load and Save pages use openQ4's page state and verbs unchanged
(`PublishRetainedLoadPage`, `HandleRetainedLoadRequest` and the Save
equivalents): 64 rows of `load.row<n>.name` and `.location`, the selected
save's `load.shot`, `load.date`, `load.time`, `load.skill`, `load.played` and
`load.error`, and `save.name`, `save.target` and `save.notice`. A save without
a picture shows Doom 3's `guis/assets/blankLevelShot`, which the stock page
now shows too (it named a Quake 4 image).

CONTROLS uses openQ4's binding route (`PublishRetainedControls`,
`HandleRetainedControlsRequest`, `RetainedControlsKey`) with Doom 3's bindings
in `RETAINED_CONTROLS_BINDINGS`, the stock page's four categories in turn; a
row is `controls_bind_<n>` and reads its keys from a `keyBinding` state. GAME
OPTIONS sets its CVars through `settings.player.set`, which accepts only the
settings the engine registers (`FindPlayerSetting`; `g_showHud` was added for
Doom 3). Both pages close with `controlsBack` and `gameBack`, which return to
the home.

SYSTEM is the settings service's page (`src/ui/SettingsService.cpp`,
`SystemSettingsHost`). The service accepts a document only when its id is
`opend3.system`, it has `onActivate` and `onBack`, it declares the service's
whole `settings.*` state (every catalog setting's draft and baseline, the
display lists, the renderer list) and it has the begin, edit, apply and
cancel operations; `page_state` in `tools/ui/doom3_menu_system.py` declares
that schema. Every video row stages a value with `settings.system.edit` (or
`settings.system.preset`, `settings.system.displayMode`); nothing changes
until APPLY CHANGES runs `settings.system.apply`. A display change then shows
the service's confirmation, whose buttons must be the nodes `settings_keep`,
`settings_revert` and `settings_retry` carrying `settings.request`. Volume
and Reverse Channels are outside the service's catalog and set their CVars at
once through `settings.player.set`.

Doom 3's four video qualities step through four of the engine's performance
presets: low, balanced, quality and ultra. A draft on another preset shows
that preset's own name. Screen Size steps the display's modes in exclusive
full screen and otherwise shows the window's size.

Create Server uses openQ4's route with Doom 3's tables at `OPEND3` sites.
`RETAINED_CREATE_GAMETYPES` is deathmatch, Tourney, Team DM, Last Man and
CTF, the last offered only where the loaded game's `si_gameType` lists it;
a value the page does not offer (`singleplayer` after a campaign) shows as
the first, and CREATE writes the shown type before it starts the server.
`RETAINED_CREATE_SETTINGS` is the stock page's and its advanced dialog's
settings. A yes-or-no row is a `toggle` control and a limit a `number`
field, each sending `<verb> <value>` through `session.menuValue`; the name,
the two passwords and the map cycle are text fields that write their page
state and then ask (`createName` and the like). The session names the
values the page shows as text (`create.gametype_name`, `create.rate_name`,
`create.lan_name`) from the stock choice strings. A map row is
`map-<n>`; its Accept sets `create.pick` and asks `createMap`.

Multiplayer Settings needs the session for two things only: the data rate's
name, which the stock keeps in one choice string (`#str_04223`), and the
player's name. The engine's default rate (128000) is none of the stock's
three, so the panel shows it as its number and keeps it as a fourth step.
Always Run and the skin are CVar states of the document, set through
`settings.player.set` (`in_alwaysRun` and `ui_skin` were registered in
`FindPlayerSetting`); a swatch is `mpset_skin_<name>` and marks itself by
comparing `ui_skin` with its own skin.

Server Type and Dedicated are not CVars in Doom 3. The stock page keeps
them in the main menu's state (`server_type`, `dedicated`), and
`StartMenuServer` now reads them there for both pages; upstream read two
Quake 4 CVars that openD3 does not have, so the stock page's two rows did
nothing. The retained page writes the same state (`createLan`,
`createDedicated`).

The session's own click (`main_menu_selection` upstream) plays
`guisounds_menuclickup`. Document fonts resolve to Doom 3's faces: `english`
(the default), `bank`, `an`, `micro` and `bigchars`.

## The generator

`tools/ui/build_doom3_menus.py` writes the documents.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_kit.py` holds the shared parts: the tokens of section 4, the
hairline, the nav button, the sub-menu item (which is also the dialog button),
the footer link, the dialog shell, the hint line and the edge glow.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_options.py` holds the option row plate, the stepping
choice, the slider, the binding row, the category tab, the apply bar and the
two options pages. `tools/ui/doom3_menu_system.py` holds the SYSTEM page: the
group box, the stepping rows over the service's draft and its two dialogs.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_credits.py` holds the Credits page and the Mods panel.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_death.py` holds the death screen's choices and their
typing.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_loading.py` holds the loading screen: its bands, bar,
typed word and text block.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_mpmenu.py` holds the in-game multiplayer menu: the
strip, the tab, the panel recipe, the filled button, the toggle, the text
link, the list row and its well, and the six panels. Its Multiplayer,
Controls and Options panels reuse the title's and the options pages' rows.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_multiplayer.py` holds the two multiplayer pages: their
frame, title and tabs, the browser's column header, radio toggle and server
row, Create Server's yes-or-no, stepping and typed rows, its map list and
its advanced dialog, and the title's Multiplayer Settings panel with its
skin swatches.
`tools/ui/doom3_menu_pages.py` holds the page parts and the two pages: the page
plate with its bridge and flair, the small glow button, the list row and its
well, the scroll bar and the name field. The home's geometry is the atlas's;
the page plates are the stock open-state rectangles.

```
python tools/ui/build_doom3_menus.py           # write the documents
python tools/ui/build_doom3_menus.py --check   # fail when one is stale
```

The outputs under `content/baseod3/pak0/guis/menu/` are generated sources:
edit the scripts and regenerate. The inherited `build_retained_screens.py` and
its modules still describe Quake 4's menus and do not run here.

Points that are easy to get wrong:

- A vector node draws nothing outside its own box. A hairline on a box's edge
  needs a node grown by its glow (`outset`, `outset_box`).
- A timeline starts from the property's current value, so its first key is
  never shown. A jump (the outline's flash) is a 1 ms step.
- A control's feedback reaches only its own subtree. The hint line therefore
  follows the document's detail subject and switches without the stock wipe.
- Up to textscale 0.30 the stock draws its 12 point atlas, taller and wider
  than the outline. `typeface` asks for the same fit: Default at 1.12 times the
  em with 0.10 em of letter spacing, AN at 1.14 and 0.08, Bank at 1.08.
- Text content must be a localization key. The documents use the stock keys
  from the installation.
- Only buttons take a `detail` (a hint); a text field that names one is
  rejected and the whole document with it.
- In a console script a control id with a hyphen before a digit needs quotes
  (`opend3_retainedGui focus "row-1"`): the tokenizer splits it otherwise.
- `session.menuValue` takes only the control's own value, so a setting that
  sends one is a `toggle`, `number`, `slider` or `choice` control. A button
  carries none: a stepping row asks with a verb of its own.
- The multiplayer pages' tabs are the nav button leaning the other way, as
  the page's frame is the title's mirrored to the top.
- The session queues `SpawnServer` behind whatever the console buffer still
  holds. A test script that waits after pressing CREATE keeps the server
  from starting until the script ends; press it last and capture the
  window.
- A property a binding sets needs a base value on its node (`display` on a
  node whose display is bound), or the document is rejected.
- A binding may set a length or a transform from a number expression
  (`width`, `top`, `[tx, ty, sx, sy, rotation]`): the loading screen places
  its band and its text from numbers the session publishes.
- At textscale 0.40 the stock draws a larger atlas than at 0.25, about 7 %
  taller than the outline and wider set; `face_40` in the loading module
  holds the fit measured for Micro and Default.
- Capture a screen no console command can reach (a load, a message box)
  by asking the client's own window to paint itself (`PrintWindow`). Never
  grab the desktop: it returns whatever is in front of the window.
- A hand-off to a stock window is a whole click: its `onAction` and then
  its `onActionRelease`. Doom 3's in-game menu switches its tabs on the
  release.
- The game's own menu opens in a script with `opend3_assertMenuActivation
  500 game` (the plain form opens the main menu). It is a cheat command,
  so a test server needs `net_allowCheats 1`.
- A test window takes the keyboard when it opens, and a key the person at
  the machine then presses reaches it (one became a binding in a test
  profile). Start it without activating it (`STARTUPINFO` with
  `SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE`).

## Verified

Windows x64, OpenGL, debugoptimized, the staged client launched windowed with
`ui_retained 1` against the Steam installation. Screens were driven with
`opend3_retainedGui` (pointer, focus, menu accept, event) and captured with the
engine's `screenshot`; the message box, which blocks the console, was captured
from the window.

- The title at 1280x960, 1280x720 and 1680x720, and at 720x960. Against a stock
  capture at 1280x720 the frame, the nav band, the OPTIONS tab and stub, the
  footer links and the label widths agree.
- Hover on LOAD GAME lights its outline and plate and writes its hint.
- NEW GAME opens the difficulty list with MARINE focused and NIGHTMARE locked.
  Choosing MARINE loads `game/mars_city1`.
- In that game the pause presents, the quit-game dialog opens and closes, and
  RETURN TO GAME returns to the game with nothing left over it.
- OPTIONS hands off to the stock options list; closing it brings the retained
  home back. CREDITS hands off to the stock credits.
- The exit dialog opens with NO focused.
- A message box with one button presents over the dimmed title. Its title and

## Open

- **Credits.** Only the id Software sequence plays; the stock page's second
  list (Doom 3 Assistance) and the button that switches to it are not there,
  because the stock menu fills that list's lines from script as it plays. The
  planet does not zoom and the logo does not drift (the stock menu draws the
  scene, and it is at rest); the pairs loop after the last one instead of
  ending. The mods panel shows a mod's name and version but not its author,
  date or directory, which the session publishes.
- **In-game multiplayer menu.** System is still the stock menu's page;
  once that tab hands off, the stock menu keeps the screen until it
  closes. Controls shows the main menu's four categories, not the stock
  panel's Multiplayer category. In New Vote the game type, kick, Next Map,
  Restart, Spectators and Time Limit votes were not called; a list holds
  16 rows; a limit outside the server's range is refused by the game with
  a chat line, as on the stock panel. The card is on the centred canvas, not anchored to
  the top-leading corner as rule 7 asks, so that a hand-off does not move
  the strip. The frame line does not flicker and the labels have no
  shadow.
- **Multiplayer.**
  Multiplayer Settings leaves out the stock CD key row (openD3 has no
  keys) and sets its three rows half a pitch higher for it; it does not dim
  MULTIPLAYER while it is open, and it opens and closes without the
  plate's unfolding. Create Server leaves out the stock PunkBuster row (a
  discontinued service); its passwords show in clear, as the stock fields
  do; a refused number (out of range) stays in its field with no message;
  and it asks for Quake 4's generic level shot when it opens, which Doom 3
  lacks (a logged warning, nothing drawn). The browser leaves out the stock
  page's filters, its connect-by-address button, its favourites, the Game
  column and EXIT GAME, and the session's status and notice lines, whose
  strings (Quake 4 keys such as `#str_107293`) Doom 3's language files lack.
  A sorted column is not marked. The page does not pan in as the stock
  multiplayer page does: the planet stays where the stock scene has it.
- **The master server is Quake 4's.** `IDNET_HOST` in
  `src/framework/licensee.h` is still `q4master.idsoftware.com`, inherited
  from openQ4, and it answers: an internet server started from either Create
  Server page announces itself there, and the browser's INTERNET source asks
  it. Which master openD3 should use is undecided.
- **Death screen.** Load leaves for the title's Load Game page, as openQ4
  routed it, where the stock screen opens a load panel of its own in hell's
  colours (section 9.8); Back from that page is the title, not the death
  screen. The choices play no sound under the pointer (the stock pings),
  and nothing has the focus when the screen opens, so a keyboard or a
  controller must move once before Accept does anything.
- **Loading screens.** The first level's screen (black, the word and bar
  centred), the hell level's (no bands, the word in red) and the fallback
  stay stock. The word's first typed frame, a lone L, is skipped: it has
  no string of its own. The bar's frame is square-cornered lines where the
  stock art is slightly rounded.
- **DEFAULTS.** The dialog has no stock fallback: it is part of the home
  documents, so it presents wherever the home does. It was exercised from
  the title only.
- **SYSTEM.** The page offers the stock page's settings, not the service's
  whole catalog: no display or refresh-rate choice, no borderless or desktop
  full screen, no window size fields, no renderer or device choice, no
  interface or text scale, and none of the engine's post effects. The stock
  Speakers and EAX rows and the ADVANCED OPTIONS dialog are folded away:
  shadows, specular, bump maps, vertical sync and antialiasing are rows on
  the page, and speakers and EAX are not offered. Dialog buttons whose only
  strings are sentence case (Discard, Keep editing, Keep, Revert, Retry) are
  set in Bank, which draws them as small capitals.
- **Options pages.** Player Name's field and the mouse sensitivity slider were
  not exercised by hand; a slider shows its number beside the ruler, which
  the stock does not; a category change does not flash its rows (section
  8.4); the stock CONTROLS page's two choice rows among the bindings (Free
  Look, Invert Mouse) are on GAME OPTIONS only; and the pages have no plate
  morph from one to another (each closes to the list).

  only the selected save's date and time. The list's sort and filter verbs

  a page is open. More than 64 saves page through NEWER SAVES and OLDER
  SAVES rows, which were not exercised.

  8.4 measures, and the list closes without motion. The hint line has no wipe.
